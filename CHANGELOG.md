# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (unreleased)

The first release: how a 5-inch FPV drone works, part by part, around a 3D model you can turn
and click.

- **What is an FPV drone?** An intro for complete beginners: what FPV means, the kit, and how
  this app is laid out.
- **The parts.** Eleven lesson sheets, from the frame to the extras: what each part does, how it
  works, what its numbers mean and what it connects to. Each opens on the drone, with its
  connections lit and the rest faded, and shows a photo of a real part (credited, from Wikimedia
  Commons).
- **How it works together.** The control, power and video paths, stop by stop, with a leader
  line across the drawing; how a quad moves, with virtual sticks and the motor speeds; and what
  happens when things go wrong (failsafe, a prop on backwards, the wrong battery, a VTX without
  its antenna).
- **The gear you hold and wear.** The radio, the goggles and the charger.
- **Progress.** Sheets you mark as read are remembered on your device; the overview says where to
  carry on.
- Works with a keyboard and a screen reader, on phones, with large text, and with reduced motion.
- Visits are counted with GoatCounter, without cookies.

Held back: Check a build, a checker for a planned build's parts, is built but switched off
