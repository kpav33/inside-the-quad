## Summary

A solid slice: the spec-driven model, the selection and focus logic, the lessons and the
architecture boundaries are clean, and every check passes. The lesson content is accurate for
beginners. One blocking issue: at 390 px the Frame lesson's spec table overflows to 452 px, so
Review Focus 4 fails (the e2e test only checks the Motors lesson). Ready to merge once that is
fixed. The should-fix items are mirrored propeller pitch in the 3D model, drei `Bounds clip`
overriding the orbit zoom limit, and the e2e drag test, which never reaches `isDeliberateClick`.

   test doesn't cover this case (finding 4).

   alert, then opens the Frame lesson from the list. The existing smoke e2e covers a failed chunk.
3. **Pressing the selected part's button again / clicking the background:** works (unit and e2e
   tests).
4. **Phone width:** **fails for the Frame lesson** (finding 1). The canvas is 422 px tall at
   390 × 844, and the lesson heading starts at y = 574, so it's visible without scrolling.
5. **Escape:** works. It closes the lesson and focuses the part button, even after a 3D
   selection (verified in the browser: focus lands on `part-button-frame`).

## Strengths

- One `DroneSpec` drives both the geometry and the lesson numbers. The lesson tests check that the
  spec rows are built from it, not retyped.
- The motor layout follows Betaflight Quad X, props in (M1 RR CW, M2 FR CCW, M3 RL CCW, M4 FL
  CW). It's covered by tests for numbering, corners, wheelbase diagonal and pairwise spin.
- Selection rules are simple and correct. The 3D click only selects, the list toggles, and the
  background, Close and Escape clear. Focus handling via a one-shot `focusTarget` ref is minimal.
- Architecture rules hold. three.js appears outside `drone-viewer`/`drone-model` only as
  `import type`; the entry chunk has no three.js; `content/` and `shared/` don't import
  `features/`; features import each other through `index.ts`, except test mocks, which the rules
  allow.
- Content check: wheelbase 225 mm, 30.5 × 30.5 stack, 2207 = 22 × 7 mm stator, 1950 KV on 6S
  (25.2 V full, ~49,000 RPM no-load: 1950 × 25.2 = 49,140), 16 × 16 mount, M5 shaft, 3 wires
  (swap two to reverse), 5 × 4.3 × 3 = diameter × pitch × blades, 127 mm, props-in as the
  Betaflight default, diagonal pairs spin the same way, a wrong or upside-down prop flips the
  drone, carbon is conductive, frame shapes and deadcat. All correct and in line with scope
  2.1–2.3.
